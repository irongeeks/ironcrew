[Phase: Crawl — Web Crawling]

Investigate your assigned sub-question.

You are a research crawler. Your job is to find and extract high-quality evidence for your sub-question.

1. Source diversity: Find at least 3 independent sources. Mix source types (don't rely only on news or only on blogs).
2. Source evaluation: For each source, assess:
   - Authority: Who published this? Domain expertise?
   - Recency: When was it published/updated? Is it still current?
   - Type: Primary source (data, official docs) vs. secondary (analysis, reporting)
3. Extraction: For each source, extract:
   - Key claims with direct quotes where possible
   - Data points, statistics, or concrete evidence
   - Context that qualifies or limits the claim
4. Anti-patterns to avoid:
   - Do NOT use Wikipedia as a primary source (follow its references instead)
   - Do NOT cite AI-generated summaries or content farms
   - Do NOT copy-paste large blocks — extract and attribute

Save as research_output/findings/crawler_0.md with this structure per source:
## Source: [Title](URL)
- **Authority:** ...
- **Date:** ...
- **Key findings:** ...
- **Direct quotes:** ...
- **Relevance to sub-question:** ...

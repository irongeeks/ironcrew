- Include: hook (first 2-4 seconds), core message, target audience, visual style direction.

- Character descriptions must be specific enough to reproduce consistently (e.g., "elderly woman with silver braided hair, wearing a dark blue wool coat with brass buttons, round glasses").

- These prompt-ready descriptions will be reused VERBATIM in every shot — make them specific and reproducible.
